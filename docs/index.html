METHODOLOGY

How the four methods choose trades

01   Risk-only

Chooses trades using risk and trading fees. The resulting tax cost is reported afterward.

02   Greedy harvesting

Uses the risk-only trades. The U.S. model selects the cheapest tax lots to sell. Canada uses one averaged ACB per security, so lot selection cannot change the result.

03   Convex heuristic

Solves a relaxed problem, fixes buy and sell directions, then checks nearby feasible allocations.

04   Four-asset benchmark

Checks all 81 buy, sell and hold direction combinations for this four-asset example. The comparison applies to the simplified continuous-share model.

In Canada, the cost basis of identical securities is averaged. Superficial-loss treatment can depend on purchases within 30 days before or after the sale and on holdings 30 days afterward, including affiliated persons. This simulation flags cases for review but cannot determine the actual tax treatment.
