MODEL TRANSPARENCY

What the optimizer does—and does not claim

01   Risk-only

Minimizes deviation from the target and explicit trading fees. It does not optimize tax-lot selection.

02   Greedy harvesting

Uses risk-only trades and the most tax-advantageous available cost basis. In Canada there is one averaged ACB pool per identical security, so there is no elective lot selection.

03   Convex heuristic

Relax the buy/sell disjunction, fix trade directions, then solve a feasible convex subproblem and test nearby directions.

04   Small global check

Enumerate all 3^4 buy/sell/hold directions, solve each convex subproblem, and compare objectives. Exact only within the simplified continuous-share model.

Canada: gains/losses are based on averaged ACB, not elective U.S. tax lots. A superficial loss may involve identical-property transactions in a 30-day window before or after the sale and day+30 ownership, including affiliated persons. Those future or linked-account facts require a separate review. The capital gains inclusion and tax rates are explicit simulation assumptions, not tax advice.
